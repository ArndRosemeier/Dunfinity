# Debug menu: level styles, and invulnerable for real

- The debug menu (the key left of 1) has a button for every level style: karst, rift, collapse, catacombs, dungeon, roots, fungal, lava, geode, basalt, flooded, dwarven and the excavation halls. It puts you inside the nearest region of that style, on this level if it has one, else on the nearest level that does (lava first turns up very deep, so that one is a long way down).
- Invulnerable now means nothing touches you at all: no red flash, no flinch or cry, no knockback, no "a rat bites: -0", and a fall does nothing either. Health never went down before, but every other sign of a hit still showed.
- The places on a level also list the nearest toadstools and puffcaps.
