# Moss, bark, plaster and roof tiles

- Rock can now be covered: moss, bark, plaster or roof tiles, drawn by the rock shader itself (no new shader builds).
- Moss grows on the floors, ledges and tops of the root caverns and between the fungal grotto's mushrooms, lush in patches, thin and broken at its edges, a few tufts up the walls.
- The roots in the root caverns are furrowed bark with pale lichen, mossy at their feet.
- The town's houses have weathered, cracked plaster walls in their own colours, plinths of dressed stone and roofs of overlapping clay or slate tiles.
