# Salt Sight as an aura

- Grandma's Salt Sight no longer draws a thin line round a creature: it lights an aura of salt round it, brightest at the creature and fading with the distance from it.
- Its edge flows slowly round the creature like a liquid, its light breathes and drifts round it in brighter and dimmer patches, and currents of salt run round it.
- Now and then a mote of salt comes off the aura and drifts away, fading.
- The colours still tell how much of a fight it is; a kind you have never brought down gets a dimmer aura in a muted colour, its salt broken and wavering.
- With reduced motion asked for, the aura holds still and sheds no motes.
