# The cellar under the house

- Grandma's cellar now lies under the whole of her house, its brick vault 0.5 m below the hall floor, instead of in a vault beside it.
- The way down is a normal turned staircase: a flight down from the hall's east end, a half-landing, and a second flight that comes down at the cellar's east end, with the cellar door at its foot.
- The low doorway, the round room, the broken arch, the passage and the old shaft all moved west with the cellar: the strange room sits just east of the house, and the way down still leads to the old shaft.
- Everything in the rooms moved with them: her shelves of jars and the crates, the bulb under the vault, the armchair with the tin, the side table with her lamp and the knife block, the notary's letter, the miner's lamp by the shaft, the ward and the salt circle.
