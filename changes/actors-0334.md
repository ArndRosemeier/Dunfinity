# People on Actors 0.33.4, and the newest AssetGenerator

- Actors 0.33.4 (from 0.8.2): skin with flush, pores and backlit ears, loose clothes that swing as people move and stop, inner layers tucked under outer ones, people far away drawn in one piece each, and many fixes.
- People of today now keep to Actors' own theme tags: modern clothes and vintage ones. The new medieval and futuristic wardrobes, and religious symbols, stay out of the creator and the village.
- AssetGenerator brought up to date (the game only uses its core package, which gains a helper to set things on the ground).
- A skirt's shadow is cast by the same shared stand-in as the body's, so swinging skirts build no shadow shader of their own (it went over the 100 ms build budget).
