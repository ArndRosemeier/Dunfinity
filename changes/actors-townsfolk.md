# The townsfolk are Actors people

- The people walking the village are the first figures from Arnd's new Actors humans: real faces, hair and skin, in everyday clothes of today (T-shirts and jeans, polo shirts, work overalls, skirts).
- They walk with motion-captured clips, stride in step with their pace, and smile when they stop for you.
- The player, the creator and the other races still use the old humans; they move over in the next steps.
