# The town sounds like a day

- The village sounds like daylight now: birds in the hedges, a breeze, a dog far off and the odd bicycle bell, in place of the night's crickets.
- Birds call around you as you walk the streets by day; nothing sings at night or below.
- A church bell tolls across the village when you climb back up out of the depths.
- A new folk tune plays in the village, taking turns with the one you know.
