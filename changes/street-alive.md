# The street comes alive

- Birds cross the sky over the village in loose flocks, wings beating.
- Smoke rises from a few of the town's chimneys, drifting on the wind and fading with height.
- A cat walks the low stone ledge by grandma's front door, sits a while, jumps down into the street and walks again.
- Villagers look up when you come close and say a line, once each time you approach; go well away and they will greet you again.
- A few of the villagers on grandma's street can be spoken to with E: they stop and face you while they answer.
