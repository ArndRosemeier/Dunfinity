# The cellar under the house, built so it shows

- Grandma's house has its floor again: over the cellar the hall, the kitchen and the living room no longer open into the cellar below, and the cellar has its brick vault over it again instead of a view up into her rooms.
- The cellar lies deeper, its floor about four metres under the hall's, so the floor between them is thick enough to be drawn; it has the height and the vault it had before it moved.
- You can walk from the cellar to the stairs again: the cellar door at the foot of the stairs sits in the cellar's east wall, and the turned stair goes up in its own walled stairwell to the hall's east end, with rails round the top and along the upper flight.
- The low doorway to her strange room is now a low passage from the cellar's north-east corner, under the kitchen, up a few stone steps to the room.
- Upstairs the living room's wall and the kitchen's door have moved a little to make room for the stairwell; the shelves, crates and barrel in the cellar stand clear of its doors.
- The cellar's walls are brick-red again (they were coloured as its floor).
- The way back that grandma's Way Home opens in the cellar stands on the cellar's floor, wherever you were when you opened it.
