# Gear with gifts, found below

- Gear can now give gifts: more health or stamina, stamina back faster, a quicker run, a higher jump, or a practice skill one step up while worn. Quality scales them, and the pack shows the numbers.
- Eight new pieces of work gear: hobnail boots, rope-soled shoes, knee pads, moleskin trousers, a leather apron, a sling, a tin of snuff and a St Barbara's medal.
- Now and then, something on the floor or in a miners' shed is a piece of gear, wrapped in sacking: the new pieces, and work gloves, helmets and tools too. Never in a rat's hoard.
- Deeper down, what you find is better more often, and more often carries a property, one gift more ("hobnail boots of the hare").
- Ilse buys found gear too.
